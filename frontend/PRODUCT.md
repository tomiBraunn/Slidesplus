# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

1. **Real users** — students, teachers, and small teams who build presentations. Bilingual (EN/ES, Rioplatense Spanish with voseo). They arrive with an idea and want a finished deck fast: AI to start, visual editing to refine, code when they need exact control.
2. **Evaluators** — recruiters, professors, and peers assessing the project as a portfolio piece. They judge polish, coherence, and craft in the first 30 seconds.

## Product Purpose

slides+ is a web app for creating presentations three ways at once: AI generation to get a structured draft, a visual editor to go deeper, and direct HTML/code access for full control. Decks render as 1920×1080 HTML sections and can be presented from a clean view link or exported.

Success looks like: a visitor understands what slides+ does within seconds of landing (by seeing it, not reading about it), and a user goes from idea to presentable deck in minutes. The app must feel like a real product, not a demo.

## Brand Personality

Clean, minimal, confident. Notion/Apple-style restraint: generous whitespace, typography carries the weight, the product is the protagonist. Dark-first interface with two brand accents — periwinkle blue `#7182FF` and green `#249931` — used sparingly, mostly for AI-related moments.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-SaaS landing grammar: uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section, numbered section markers (01/02/03) as scaffolding, identical icon+heading+text card grids, gradient text.
- Decorative glassmorphism as a default material.
- Landings that describe the product but never show it (no real decks, no editor, no output).
- Overstimulating WebGL/gradient backgrounds that compete with content.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — real decks, real templates, and the real editor are the hero content on every marketing surface.
2. **Less, but better** — few colors, lots of air, strong typographic hierarchy instead of boxes and borders.
3. **One hand** — landing and app share the same visual system; crossing from marketing to product should feel seamless.
4. **Fast to value** — minimal friction from arrival to first deck; the AI prompt input is always one step away.
5. **Both languages, same quality** — EN and ES copy are written, not translated word-by-word.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text contrast ≥4.5:1 (audit existing `text-white/40`-style low-opacity text on black).
- Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` alternative.
- Keyboard navigable: skip links, focus states, accessible accordions and modals.
- Bilingual UI (EN/ES) with language toggle.
